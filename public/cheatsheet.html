The demo — four steps, nothing else

  • 01Open the customer menu. Say: “this is what the diner sees after scanning the code on the table.”
  • 02Add two dishes to the cart and send the order.
  • 03Switch to the kitchen screen. The order is already there. Move it to cooking, then ready.
  • 04Open the owner’s dashboard. The savings counter has gone up. Stop there.

Do not walk through every page and every button. Your teacher said this exactly: he got lost in your last presentation because of too many features. Four steps. End on the money.

Words, in case he asks

Commission
The cut. The money Talabat keeps from every order before the restaurant gets paid. On a 60 dirham meal at 30%, Talabat keeps 18.
AED / dirham
The same thing. AED is just the short code for dirhams.
Flat fee
A price that never changes. 300 a month whether they sell 50 meals or 5,000. The opposite of a percentage.
Break even
The point where they stop losing and start saving. For you it is 20 orders a month. Below that you cost more than you save; above it, every order is profit for them.
Positioning
Where you sit next to your competitors. Yours: not fighting Talabat, but the escape from it.
The market
How many possible customers exist. Yours is 26,795 restaurants and cafés in the UAE.
Revenue
Money coming in. 200 restaurants × 300 dirhams = 60,000 a month.
15% of the company
If he gives you 250,000 dirhams, he owns 15 out of every 100 dirhams the company is ever worth. You keep 85.
ChatFood
A Dubai company, started 2018, that built almost the same thing as you.
Deliverect
A bigger company that bought ChatFood in May 2023. This is your proof that someone pays money for this idea.

Three questions he will probably ask

“Why doesn’t Talabat just copy you?”

They cannot. The commission is their business. If they stop taking it, they have no income. That is exactly why a separate company like ChatFood could exist for five years and then get bought.

“Why would a restaurant trust a student?”

They do not have to trust me for long. The first 30 orders every month are free, so they see it working before they pay anything. And 300 dirhams is less than one day of commission for most of them.

“What if nobody buys it?”

Then I have lost your 250,000 and a year of my life. That is exactly why the money goes to a salesperson and not to writing more code. The app is already finished. I need to find out whether restaurants say yes in month two, not month ten.

If he asks something you do not know, say “I don’t know yet, and here is how I would find out.” He marked you up last time for asking serious questions. Admitting a gap beats inventing a number he can check.

If you forget everything else

“Delivery apps take up to a third of every order, and they keep the customer’s details. MyMenu gives a restaurant its own ordering page for 300 dirhams a month, flat. They break even at 20 orders. There are 26,795 restaurants in the UAE, and a Dubai company that did this got bought in 2023. I want 250,000 dirhams for 15 percent, to pay a salesperson for a year.”

Six sentences. That is the whole pitch. Everything on the slides is just this, slower.
